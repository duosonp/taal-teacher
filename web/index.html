How Taal Teacher works

  • Pick one language to learn a feature, or several to compare them side by side.
  • Type any grammatical feature, or press Suggest topics for ideas that suit your languages.
  • Each lesson has paradigm tables, glossed examples (word by word), typical mistakes and practice.
  • Write your own sentence under Try it yourself to have it checked, or ask the teacher a follow-up.
  • The Library holds hand-checked lessons that work without any connection to Claude.
